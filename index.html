About me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Xiang Li. I received my bachelor’s degree in Automation from Tsinghua in 2021, and expect to complete my Ph.D. in June 2027.

My research focuses on learning and control for dexterous manipulation, spanning arm–hand grasp generation, tactile feedback, and manipulation at the microscale. I combine physical models, motion planning, and robot learning to enable reliable interaction in contact-rich and uncertain environments.

I am also interested in visual–tactile representation learning and tactile-aware vision–language–action (VLA) policies, especially how touch can support multimodal understanding and closed-loop dexterous manipulation in contact-rich tasks.
